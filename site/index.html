Mismatched emails

Hidden signals

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The tool is free to use with no account. Lookups run through our IP data provider, so very heavy use is rate limited to keep it fair for everyone.

Do you store my data?

No. Your CSV is parsed in your browser. Only the IP addresses are sent for lookup, and results are cached for at most 24 hours, unlinked to you. Email addresses and names never leave your device.

How accurate is the location data?

IP location is very reliable at the country level and usually good at the region level. City-level results are an estimate, and VPNs or mobile networks can place a visitor far from where they really are. Treat location as one signal, not proof.

How is the email match scored?

We compare the email domain with the company name using several strategies, from exact matches to acronyms. Scores run from 0.0 to 1.0, and the docs explain each one.

Does it detect VPNs and proxies?

It flags IPs that belong to hosting and datacenter networks, which is where most VPN and proxy traffic comes from. Full VPN, proxy, and Tor detection needs a paid IP data plan and isn't included yet.

Can I use it for work?

Yes. Commercial use is allowed under our terms.
