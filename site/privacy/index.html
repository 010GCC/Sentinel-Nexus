Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

SolunIP Labs ("we", "us") runs soluniplabs.com and the lead enrichment tool at soluniplabs.com/app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the tool is built so your lead lists stay on your device.

1. Using the tool

  • Your CSV stays in your browser. Files you upload are read and scored on your own device. Names, email addresses, company names, and other columns are never sent to us.
  • Only IP addresses are looked up. To enrich an IP, your browser sends it to our lookup service, which asks our IP data provider, IPinfo, for location and network details.
  • Short-term caching. To avoid repeat lookups, the result for a given IP address may be cached on Cloudflare's network for up to 24 hours. Cached results aren't linked to you or to the file you uploaded.
  • No accounts. The tool doesn't require signing up, and we don't build profiles of tool users.

2. Contact form

If you send us a message through the contact form, we collect the name, email address, company (optional), and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you. Messages are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, forwarded to our own email inbox, and are not added to a mailing list. You can ask us to delete your message at any time (see section 6).

3. Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request in order to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and enforce rate limits. Cloudflare may keep short-lived operational logs. We don't use advertising trackers, and the site doesn't set cookies for analytics or advertising.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the site:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security, request handling, and storing contact form messages
  • IPinfo: IP address lookups

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Your responsibilities with lead data

If you upload data about other people, such as form submissions, you're responsible for having a lawful basis to process it. Because that data stays in your browser, we never receive it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information. To make a request, email hello@soluniplabs.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Retention

We keep contact form messages for up to 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them. Cached IP lookup results expire within 24 hours.

8. Children

The site is a business tool and isn't directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@soluniplabs.com.
