Quick start

  1. Open the tool. No account or API key is needed.
  2. Choose Upload CSV and drop in an export of your form submissions, or choose Paste IPs for a simple list.
  3. Check the detected columns, then press Enrich.
  4. Filter by grade, then press Download CSV. The download contains your original columns plus the enrichment.

Want to see it first? Press Try sample data in the tool.

Email match scoring

The tool compares the email's domain with the company name after removing legal suffixes (Inc, LLC, Ltd, GmbH, and so on), punctuation, and accents. The first strategy that matches sets the score:

ScoreStrategyExample
1.0Exact matchacme.com + "Acme Corp"
0.9One contains the otherfennerppd.com + "Fenner"
0.8Company words spell out the domain, including common abbreviationsabcmfg.com + "ABC Manufacturing"
0.75Acronymeliteap.com + "Elite Advanced Polymers"
0.7Close spelling, a shortened company name, or a free-mail username that matches the companyeni3.com + "eniwher3"
0.6Domain contains a distinctive company wordnorthwind-freight.com + "Northwind Logistics"
0.0No match, free-mail, or throwaway domainbob@gmail.com + "Acme"

Common words like "tech", "global", "group", and "solutions" don't count as a match on their own, which prevents false positives such as tech.com matching "Acme Tech".

Lead grades

Every row starts at 50 points. Signals add or subtract points, and the total sets the grade: A is 70 or more, B is 45 to 69, and C is below 45.

SignalPoints
Corporate email that matches the company (score 0.75 or higher)+40
Corporate email with a partial match (0.6 to 0.74)+25
Corporate email that doesn't match+5
Free-mail address (username matches the company: +15)+5

Hosting or datacenter IP−25
VPN, proxy, or Tor IP (when your data plan reports it)−30

Grades are a triage aid. A C-grade lead can still be real, for example someone on a work VPN, so review before deleting anything.

Network types

  • business: the IP belongs to an organization's own network.
  • isp: a consumer or business internet provider such as Comcast or Spectrum.
  • mobile: a cellular carrier.
  • hosting: a cloud or datacenter provider such as AWS or DigitalOcean. Real people rarely browse from these, so they often mean bots, scrapers, or VPNs.
  • anonymizer: a known VPN, proxy, or Tor exit. Only shown when the IP data includes privacy detection.

Network type is inferred from the network owner's name unless the IP data includes an explicit type.

Limits and privacy

  • Your file never leaves your browser. Only IP addresses are sent for lookup.
  • Lookups are limited to 60 requests per minute per visitor. The tool batches IPs, so this rarely matters.
  • Private and reserved addresses (such as 10.x.x.x or 192.168.x.x) can't be looked up.
  • City-level location is an estimate. Country is the most reliable field.

API

The lookup service the tool uses is available for light use from soluniplabs.com pages:

GET  /api/lookup?ip=8.8.8.8
POST /api/batch   {"ips": ["8.8.8.8", "1.1.1.1"]}   (up to 100 per request)

Responses are IPinfo-style JSON. Need higher volume or an integration with your CRM? Get in touch.

Troubleshooting

"Pick at least an IP column or an Email column"

The tool couldn't recognize your headers. Choose the right columns in the dropdowns that appear after you load the file.

Every lookup fails

Some corporate networks block outside lookups. Try from another network. If the Error column says "rate limited", wait a minute and run again.

Scores look wrong for a company

Email us the email domain and company name (no personal details needed) at hello@soluniplabs.com and we'll tune the matching.
